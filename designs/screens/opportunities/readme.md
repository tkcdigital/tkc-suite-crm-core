# opportunities screen

## Oracle Service Cloud

The OSC counterpart of the suiteCRM opportunities entity is **Opportunity**

A commercial lead or potential sales deal tracked by sales representatives.
Expected revenue, win probability percentage, estimated close date, and sales stages (*Lead*, *Active*, *Inactive*, *Won*, *Lost*).
Associated with a primary **Contact** and/or an **Organization**; assigned to a specific sales representative.
Sales pipeline management, forecasting, and tracking associated products and quotes.
