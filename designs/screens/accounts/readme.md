# accounts screen

## Oracle Service Cloud

The OSC counterpart of the suiteCRM accounts entity is **Organization** 
A company, public institution, or business unit with which business is conducted. | Supports **parent-child hierarchies** (subsidiaries and parent companies). | Can be linked to multiple **Contacts**; **Incidents** and **Opportunities** can be associated directly or via linked contacts. | Manages organization-wide **Service Level Agreements (SLAs)**, contracts, and provides a consolidated view of all interactions across all employees. |
