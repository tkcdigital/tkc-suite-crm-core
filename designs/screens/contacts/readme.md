# contacts screen

## Oracle Service Cloud

The OSC counterpart of the suiteCRM contact entity is **Contact** 
An individual (consumer, customer, or employee of an organization) with whom interaction occurs. Personal details (name, postal address), multiple email addresses, phone numbers, and channel IDs (chat, social media).
Can be linked to an **Organization** (B2B) or exist independently (B2C); acts as the primary requester of an **Incident** or contact for an **Opportunity**.
Tracks communication history, communication channel preferences, and opt-ins for marketing mailings.
